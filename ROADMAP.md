## How items move through this roadmap

```
💡 Considering → 📅 Planned → 🛠️ In progress → 🟢 Recently shipped
```

Items in **Considering** are open to lobbying via Discussions. Items in **Planned** are committed. Items in **In progress** typically have an open issue or PR. Items in **Recently shipped** are in the latest few releases.

---

## How to propose a new roadmap item

1. **Search existing issues and discussions** first — your idea may already be tracked.
2. Open a [💡 Feature Request issue](https://github.com/aliansoftwareteam/AlianHub-Project-Management-System/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) describing the problem you're trying to solve (not just the solution).
3. Maintainers will triage and respond with either:
   - "Accepted — moving to Considering / Planned"
   - "Needs more discussion — moving to Discussions"
   - "Out of scope — declining with reasoning"
