# 5. Working with people

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Select **Invite**.
3. Type one or more email addresses.
4. Choose a **Role**:
   - **Guest** can look around and comment. Cannot create or change anything.
   - **Member** works on projects and tasks day to day.
   - **Admin** can do everything except billing and deleting the company.
5. Select **Send**.

If the email cannot be sent, AlianHub shows a join link to copy and send yourself. Pending people show as "invited" with a **resend** link.

The **Invite** button shows only if your role allows inviting.

An invited person opens the link, sets a name and password, then logs in. Being invited puts them in the workspace. It does not put them in a project.

## Assign a task

Open the task. In **Details**, select **Assignee** and pick a person. You only see people who can work on that project.

## Comment

Open the task, find **Activity**, and select **Comments**. Type your note. Enter sends it. Shift and Enter start a new line.

## Mention someone

In a comment, type `@`. A list titled **People to mention** opens. Keep typing a name, then pick it. The person gets it in their [Inbox](06-inbox.md).
