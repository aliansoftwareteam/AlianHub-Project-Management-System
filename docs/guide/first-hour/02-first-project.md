# 2. Your first project

A project is one piece of work with a goal, such as a launch or a client job.

Start from **Projects** in the left bar, then select **+ New project**. On Home, **Create a project** does the same.

You have three ways to begin.

## Start blank

1. In the **New project** window, leave **Blank** chosen. It gives you three statuses, To Do, In Progress and Done, and nothing else.
2. Type a **Project name** of at least 3 characters.
3. A short **Key** fills in from the name. It must not match another project's key.
4. Choose **Visibility**: everyone in your workspace, or only people you add.
5. Choose a **Source**. The screen needs one before it lets you go on.
6. Select **Create project**.

## Start from a template or the sample

- Pick a template in the left column of the same window. Each shows its statuses and how many sample tasks it brings.
- If you kept the sample project on in step 1, you already have **Welcome to AlianHub**. It has a **SAMPLE** badge. Read it, then remove it when you are done with **Remove sample data**.
- A template with sample tasks has a tick box to include them.

## Tell your AI app what you need

- In the **New project** window, **Create from a description** asks you to describe the project. It drafts the lists and tasks for you to review. You see this option only when AI is switched on for you.
- If you connected an outside AI app (see [Connect your AI app](08-connect-ai.md)), say "Set up my project". It asks a few questions and shows a plan. Nothing is made until you say yes.

## What you see after

A new blank project opens on the List view and says "This project has no tasks yet". Next: [Lists, folders and tasks](03-lists-folders-tasks.md).
