# 8. Connect your AI app

You can connect the AI app you already use, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Then you can ask it in everyday words to find, add and change work for you. It sees and changes only what you can.

Open **AI** in the left bar, then **Connect your AI**. This page also opens after you create a workspace, as **One last step**. You can select **Skip for now**.

The full steps, what it can and cannot do, and what waits for your approval are in [Connect your own AI to AlianHub](../../CONNECT-YOUR-AI.md). They are not repeated here.
