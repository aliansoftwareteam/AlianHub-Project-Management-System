# 6. The Inbox

The Inbox is the place that tells you what needs you. Select **Inbox** in the left bar. A number on it shows what is unread.

## The tabs

| Tab | What is in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs your approval** | Time-off requests and changes proposed by an AI. Often empty. |
| **Primary** | New things for you: mentions, comments given to you, replies, reminders. It opens first. |
| **Other** | Updates on things you only watch. |
| **Later** | Things you set aside with **Snooze**. |
| **Done** | Things you marked done or opened. |
| **Cleared** | Things you cleared. They are deleted after 30 days. |

**Filter** narrows a tab to Mentions, Assigned comments, Reminders or Updates.

## What to do with a row

- **Open task** goes to it.
- **Reply here** answers without leaving.
- **Mark done** moves it to Done.
- **Snooze** brings it back later, such as Tomorrow or Next week.
- **Clear** removes it. **Mark all read** and **Clear all** work on the whole tab.

"You're all caught up" on Primary means nothing needs you.
