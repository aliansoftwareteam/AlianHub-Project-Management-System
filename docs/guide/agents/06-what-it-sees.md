# What your AI can see

## Only what you can open

Your AI acts as you. It sees what you can open in AlianHub, and nothing more.

- If you cannot open a project, your AI cannot read it or change it.
- A list that is hidden from you is hidden from your AI.
- Someone else's personal list is not shown to it. This holds even if you are an owner or admin.
- A doc is read if you can open it, or if it was shared with you by name.
- A change is made only if your role lets you make it.

If you made a token and chose certain projects, your AI sees only those projects.

## Chat only if you allow it

Your AI reads no chat unless you ticked **Read chat** when you connected it. See [Connect your AI app](01-connect.md).

With it, your AI can see:

- The chat channels you are in.
- The recent messages of a channel, or of a task's thread, that you can open.

It never reads your direct messages. A token kept to certain projects reads no chat channels at all.

## After it reads

What your AI app reads goes into your chat with that app. From then on, the app's own rules apply to it.
