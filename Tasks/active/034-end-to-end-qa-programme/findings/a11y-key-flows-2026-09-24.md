# Accessibility pass on the everyday flows (task 034)

Finding prefix `A11Y`. WCAG 2.2 AA, beyond colour contrast: text contrast and the `--ink-3` retirement
are handled in `fix/retire-ink-3-for-text`, so `color-contrast` is switched off in every check here and
no text-grey token was touched.

**Flows.** Sign in; Home; a project's List and Board; the task detail overlay (title, status, assignee,
due date, a comment with an @mention); creating a task; Inbox; Settings → Members; Ask (AI). Also the
first-run tour, because it covers Home on a new account.

**Method.** The e2e harness (`e2e/support/harness.js`) on its own Mongo (port 27218) with the standard
fixture workspace, Chrome through Playwright at 1280 × 720, as the owner, light and dark.
`@axe-core/playwright` 4.13 (added as a dev dependency) ran on each screen; the first pass used axe's
default rule set, and the verification pass added the WCAG 2.2 tags, which is where `target-size` came
in. Keyboard behaviour was checked by driving Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space and Escape and reading
`document.activeElement` after each key. Focus visibility was checked by comparing each Tab stop's
computed outline, box-shadow and border with and without focus, then by eye on close-up captures.
Screenshots are not committed.

**Guard.** `e2e/specs/a11y.spec.js` fails on any serious or critical axe violation (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A
and AA, contrast excluded) on each flow above, and asserts the keyboard paths: Board reachable and a
card opened from the keyboard, focus kept in the task overlay for 40 Tabs and returned to the row on
Escape, status changed through its picker, due-date calendar opened. Unit tests:
`frontend/tests/unit/useFocusTrap.spec.js` and `frontend/tests/unit/a11yKeyFlows.spec.js`.
