# 1. Sign up and set up

A workspace is the place your team's projects live. It is usually your company or your team. This page shows the three ways you arrive in one: as the first person on a new install, through an invitation, or with an account that has no workspace yet.

## If you are the first person on a new install

1. Open the address of your AlianHub. On a new install it opens **Set up AlianHub**.
2. Fill in First name, Last name, Email and Password. The password needs an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol.
3. Type a **Workspace name**.
4. Pick what your team mainly does. This only chooses which sample project and templates come first. You can change it later.
5. Leave **Start with a sample project** ticked if you want something to look at. You can remove it later.
6. Select **Create workspace**.

You are signed in as the owner of the install. You do not need to check your email.

## If someone invited you

You do not make a workspace. Open the link in your invitation. It says "You're joining" and the workspace name. Fill in your name and password and select **Continue**. Then log in. See [Bring people in](./people).

## If you made your account somewhere else

If you have an account but no workspace, AlianHub asks you to **Name your workspace**.

1. Type a name. Use at least 3 characters.
2. Pick a **Team size** and select **Continue**.
3. Pick what your team mainly does, then select **Open my workspace**. To start with nothing in it, choose **Skip — start blank** instead.

If AlianHub says "Verify your email first", open the link we sent you, then log in. **Resend email** sends it again.

## What you see next

A page called **One last step** asks you to connect an AI app. You can select **Skip for now** and do it later. See [Connect your AI app](./connect-ai). Then you land on **Home**.
