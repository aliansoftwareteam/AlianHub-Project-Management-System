# 8. Connect your AI app

You can connect the AI app you already use, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Then you can ask it in everyday words to find, add and change work for you. It sees and changes only what you can, and changes that need a look wait for your approval in the Inbox.

## Where to start

Open **AI** in the left bar, then **Customize**, and choose the **Connect your AI** tab. This page also opens after you create a workspace, as **One last step**. You can select **Skip for now** and come back later.

## The rest of the steps

The full steps for each AI app, what it can and cannot do, and what waits for your approval are in the "Connect your AI" section. They are not repeated here.

- [Connect your AI app](/ai/connect): the steps, app by app
- [What it can do](/ai/what-it-can-do)
- [Approve in the Inbox](/ai/approve-in-the-inbox)
- [Things to say](/ai/things-to-say): sentences to try first

::: tip A good first sentence
Once connected, try "Set up my project". Your AI app asks a few questions and shows a plan. Nothing is made until you say yes.
:::
