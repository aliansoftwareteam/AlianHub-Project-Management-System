# Your first hour with AlianHub

This path is for someone who has never used a project tool. Each page takes a few minutes. Do them in order the first time, and by the end you will have a workspace, a project with tasks, your team invited and time logged.

Every button name on these pages is the word the screen shows. If a button is missing on your screen, your role may not allow it. Ask the person who owns your workspace.

| Step | Page | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Sign up and set up](./sign-up) | Make your account and name your workspace |
| 2 | [Your first project](./first-project) | Start a project, blank or from a template |
| 3 | [Lists, folders and tasks](./lists-folders-tasks) | Add tasks, open one, change its status |
| 4 | [Views](./views) | Look at the same tasks as a list, a board, a calendar |
| 5 | [Bring people in](./people) | Invite, assign, comment and mention |
| 6 | [The Inbox](./inbox) | See what needs you |
| 7 | [Log time](./time) | Track time and fill in your timesheet |
| 8 | [Connect your AI app](./connect-ai) | Let Claude or ChatGPT work in AlianHub for you |
| 9 | [Where things are](./where-things-are) | A map of the left bar |

::: tip Try it without installing
You can follow along on the live demo at [demo.alianhub.com](https://demo.alianhub.com) before you set up your own.
:::
