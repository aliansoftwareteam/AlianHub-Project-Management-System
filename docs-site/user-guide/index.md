# User guide

This guide is for people who use AlianHub to manage work: project leads, team members, and the people who approve time and look at reports. Each page covers one part of the product in depth: what it is for, how to use it step by step, and what it cannot do.

If you have never used AlianHub, start with [Your first hour](/getting-started/first-hour/) and come back here when you want the detail. If a button is missing on your screen, your role may not allow it. See [People and roles](./people-and-roles).

| [Templates](./templates) | Reuse projects, tasks and views as starting points. |
